Inside the player

Features

01

Music that never stops

Crossfade: the next track starts while the last one fades out. Silence at the start and end of a file is skipped automatically. Levelled volume: every track is analysed once so it plays at the same perceived loudness as the rest.

02

Shape your sound

10-band equalizer: adjust the response curve with a single gesture. 3D spatial audio: an immersive render for headphones that places the sound stage around you.

03

Smart transitions

Omega Player measures the tempo and the tone of your tracks to line up a queue that holds together instead of killing the mood. You keep full control to reorder it at any time.

04

A library that keeps itself tidy

Automatic playlists: dynamic sorting by genre, with no effort from you. Sort your way: by recently added, recently played, length, title or tempo.

05

Make it yours

29 visual moods, between animated backgrounds and foreground effects.

Zero tracking: no profile, no usage statistics, no identifier collected.

No server: the app contains no remote server address at all and works entirely without a network. Crash reports stay on your device.

You stay in charge of the source

Omega Player has no catalogue and wants none: it plays your files, full stop. Nothing is installed for you, nothing runs behind your back, nothing nudges you towards a service.

And if you do want to plug an online source into it, you pick the extension, you install it, and it answers to you alone.

A pricing model you can read in one line

The app is 100% free and already includes all 29 visual moods — kept for life by early adopters.

The entire playback engine (spatial audio, equalizer, playlists, queue, Android Auto, Wear OS) will stay free, with no restriction. Only five exclusive visual moods, offered later on, will ever be a paid option.
